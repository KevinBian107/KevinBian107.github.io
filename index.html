Hi, I’m Kaiwen. I design artificial systems that harness natural intelligence.

I am a research staff at Enigma, Stanford University, and I recently graduated from UC San Diego. My research interests lie in representation alignment and steering, particularly for world modeling and AI4Research.

During my undergrad, I was fortunate to be advised by Talmo D. Pereira at the Salk Institute, Scott W. Linderman at Stanford University, and Yusu Wang and Gal Mishne at the Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute. Outside of my academic life, I enjoy skateboarding 🛹, surfing 🏄, and immersing myself in the serenity of the natural world 🌲
